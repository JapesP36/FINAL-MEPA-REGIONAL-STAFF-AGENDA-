Final Team Agenda · 4–6 September 2026

MEPA Regional Staff Strategy Summit

A focused working summit to create greater clarity on what matters most, how we work together, what we need to put in place, and what each of us will own after the summit.

Our summit journey

Focus — What matters most?
Work — How must we work together?
Build — What must we put in place?
Commit — What will we own?

This is not a new regional strategy process. The Regional Council has already set the strategic direction for MEPA. Our work is to translate that direction into a simple, practical way of operating as a Regional Staff Team.

The pre-summit survey gives us useful context, but we will not work through every survey question or attempt to solve every issue raised. The survey will help us focus on the areas that matter most.

By the end of the summit, we want greater operational clarity, stronger team collaboration, better continuity of work, clear ownership and a practical roadmap that we can immediately begin implementing.

Overall Architecture

Three days. Four working sessions. Four clear outputs.

Each session is built around one simple question. The aim is not to create more complexity, but to leave with decisions and tools that make our work easier, clearer and more sustainable.

Session 1 · Focus

What Must We Focus On?

Start with the direction already set for the region, use the survey as context, and agree the few priorities around which the Regional Staff Team needs to organise.

Friday, 4 September13:30–16:30 · 3 hours

Purpose

Create shared clarity on what matters most for the Regional Staff Team and what the summit needs to help us achieve.

Core question What are the few things we need to get right as a Regional Staff Team?

What we will focus on

  • A concise view of what we learned from the pre-summit survey.
  • The Regional Council's existing direction and what it means for Regional Staff.
  • The few priorities that need our collective attention.
  • What belongs in this summit versus what should be addressed later through team development or operational improvement.
Session output
A short list of 3–5 agreed Regional Staff priorities, each with a clear statement of what success should look like.
Session 2 · Work

How Must We Work Together?

Focus on the practical behaviours and operating clarity that will help us function as one team and reduce dependence on individual people.

Saturday, 5 September09:00–12:00 · 3 hours

Purpose

Agree the simple ways of working that will strengthen accountability, collaboration, knowledge sharing and business continuity.

Core question What do we need from one another for this team to work effectively, even when someone is unavailable?

Four areas we will keep the conversation around

OwnershipWhere does responsibility sit, and where do we need greater clarity?
CommunicationWhat information needs to flow across the team and how do we keep one another informed?
CollaborationWhere do we rely on one another, and how do we support delivery across functions?
ContinuityWhat must exist so important work does not stop when someone is unavailable or leaves?
Session output
One practical Regional Staff Team Agreement covering how we own work, communicate, collaborate, support one another and protect continuity.
Session 3 · Build

What Do We Need to Put in Place?

Convert the priorities and team agreement into practical actions rather than designing large or complicated new systems.

Saturday, 5 September14:00–17:00 · 3 hours

Purpose

Build a simple implementation roadmap that identifies the actions, owners and support needed after the summit.

Core question What must we actually put in place after this summit?

What we will work through

  • The practical actions required to move each agreed priority forward.
  • Who will own or lead each action.
  • What support or input will be required from others.
  • Where we are currently too dependent on one person and what simple step can reduce that dependency.
  • Which useful survey ideas belong in a longer-term development or improvement list rather than the immediate roadmap.
Session output
A simple Regional Staff Action Roadmap showing What · Owner · By When · Support Needed, plus a short list of future development areas to address after the summit.
Session 4 · Commit

What Are We Committing To?

Bring the work together, confirm ownership and leave with a clear understanding of what happens next.

Sunday, 6 September09:00–11:00 · 2 hours

Purpose

Close the summit with clarity, accountability and momentum rather than another layer of discussion.

Core question What are we leaving with, and how will we make sure it happens?

What we will confirm

  • Our agreed Regional Staff priorities.
  • Our Team Agreement.
  • The Action Roadmap and named owners.
  • The immediate actions that happen first.
  • How and when we will review progress.
  • One personal commitment from each team member.
Session output
A clear set of final commitments and next steps, including owners, first actions, a review rhythm and individual commitments.
